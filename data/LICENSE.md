- **Basemap tiles** are built from Protomaps, an ODbL-licensed OpenStreetMap derivative (same terms).
- **Elevation (contours, hillshade)** is derived from USGS 3DEP data, which is in the U.S. public domain.
  Credit: U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program.
- **Kitsap County park locations** (`data/raw/kitsap_park_locations/`) are redistributed under the
  Kitsap County Open Data terms of use recorded in `data/sources.yaml` (`kitsap_open_data_hub`), not ODbL.
- **Agency names and attributes** (Bainbridge Island Metro Park & Recreation District, City of
  Bainbridge Island, Kitsap County) are used as facts for naming and cross-checks; their geometry is
  not redistributed here. See `data/sources.yaml` for each source's terms.

Per-source credits shipped in the app are listed in `data/ATTRIBUTION.md` (generated by `bitrails dist`).
