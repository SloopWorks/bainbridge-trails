Bainbridge Trails maps the public trails of Bainbridge Island, Washington, and keeps track of the ones you have walked. Record a walk and the app fills in the trail sections you covered. Finish a trail, then keep going until you have walked the whole island.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse more than 100 named island trails on an offline map (Topo, Light or Dark style), with length, elevation gain, park and trailheads.
• Search and filter the trail list by status: not started, partway, nearly done, complete.
• Record a walk. Recording keeps going with the screen locked.
• See which sections you covered, your progress on each trail, and your share of the whole island.
• Celebrate each trail you complete.
• Optional: sign in to back up your progress and join the island leaderboard under a display name or a generated anonymous name.
• See community stats per trail, such as how many people have walked it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your GPS track stays on your phone. Raw tracks are deleted from the phone 30 days after a walk; your progress is kept.
• If you sign in, we sync only which trail sections you have covered, never your GPS track or walk times.
• The leaderboard shows a name, island percentage and trails completed. It never shows where or when you walked.
• No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs.
• Delete your account in the app at any time.

WORKS OFFLINE
The map and trail data are built into the app, so you can browse and record without a signal. Syncing and the leaderboard need a connection.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Location is used only during a walk you start (it keeps recording with the screen off until you stop) or while the map shows your position. There is no location access at other times.
• Precise location is needed because island trails run close together.
• Optional Motion & Fitness access helps keep bike and car rides from counting as walks.
• Trail conditions change. Follow posted signs, closures and park rules.
