# Content rating and audience notes

Category: **All other app types** (utility / reference: a trail map and walk tracker; not a game, not social
media, not news).

Expected result: **Everyone** (ESRB), **PEGI 3**, USK 0, ClassInd L.

**Target audience** (App content > Target audience and content): 13–15, 16–17 and 18+. Not designed for children,
not in the Families program. Store listing has no child-directed imagery. Answer "No" to "Could your app
unintentionally appeal to children?" with the note: a trail map / walk tracker for adults and families walking
together; children can use it without an account.

## App Store (age rating questionnaire)

All content descriptors: **None**. Medical/treatment info: No. Unrestricted web access: No. Gambling / contests:
No. Expected rating: **4+**.

**User-generated content.** If asked, answer that users choose a public display name. Guideline 1.2 expects, for
UGC, (a) filtering (done: blocklist + format rules, TL-9), (b) reporting (done: Report name), (c) the ability to
block abusive users, and (d) published contact info (done: `/support`, `/privacy`). **(c) is missing:** P1-7 "hide
reported rows for the reporter immediately" is not done. Risk: App Review may ask for it. Mitigation options:
implement P1-7 before iOS submission (preferred), or explain in review notes that only short, filtered names are
shown and reported names are reviewed by an admin who can reset or hide them.

## Both stores

- Not a children's app; no ads; no IAP; no location sharing between users.
- Safety copy: listing and terms tell users to follow posted signs and closures; the app is not a navigation or
  safety device.
- Independence statement (listing + terms): not affiliated with the Bainbridge Island Metro Park & Recreation
  District or the City of Bainbridge Island. Avoids "official"/impersonation review flags (Play Impersonation
  policy, App Store 5.2).
