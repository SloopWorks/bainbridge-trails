# Bainbridge Trails support

Questions, bug reports, a trail that is missing or wrong, or a name on the leaderboard that should be reviewed:
email [privacy@sloopworks.com](mailto:privacy@sloopworks.com). Tell us your phone model and the app version
(Me > Settings, at the bottom).

## Common questions

**Why doesn't my walk count?** Walks count only for trail sections you actually walked, with precise location
on. Bike and car rides are filtered out. If physical-activity access is off, walks are saved as "Unverified".

**Do I need an account?** No. The map, recording and your progress work without one. Signing in adds backup and
the leaderboard.

**Who can see where I walked?** Nobody. Your GPS track stays on your phone; the leaderboard shows only a name,
island percentage and trails completed. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).

**How do I change my leaderboard name?** Me > Account. Without a custom name, your anonymous name is shown.

**How do I delete my account?** Me > Account > Delete account, or see the [account deletion page](/delete-account).

**A trail is wrong or missing.** Trail lines come from OpenStreetMap. Email us, or fix it at
[openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org); fixes reach the app with the next data update.
